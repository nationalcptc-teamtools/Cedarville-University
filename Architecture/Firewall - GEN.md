# Firewall - GEN

A firewall controls traffic between networks or hosts according to a security policy. It can permit or block communication based on configured rules and supported inspection capabilities.

Policy enforcement and service availability are different questions. A filtered connection does not establish that a service is absent, and an allowed connection does not establish that the service is secure.

Use [[NMAP]] observations with an understanding of filtering behavior. [[Architecture/IDS - GEN|IDS]] describes a separate monitoring and detection role. [[Architecture/Network Layer - GEN|Network Layer]], [[Architecture/Transport Layer - GEN|Transport Layer]], and [[Ports]] provide related networking concepts.
