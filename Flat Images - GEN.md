# Flat Images - GEN

Flattened images are screenshots or illustrations whose visible edits have been combined into a single image. This is useful when preparing evidence for [[Reporting]], particularly when the working image contains editable annotations or masks.

**Protecting sensitive information**
---
1. Identify credentials, tokens, personal data, and unrelated client information.
2. Remove sensitive content with an appropriate redaction operation rather than merely covering it with an editable shape.
3. Flatten the approved image before placing it in the report.
4. Remove hidden information and metadata from the final document.
5. Reopen the delivered copy and inspect the redacted areas and searchable text.

Flattening, redaction, and document sanitization are separate operations. A flat appearance does not establish that hidden PDF content, attachments, or metadata have been removed.
