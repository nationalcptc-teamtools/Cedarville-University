# Security Models

## CIA triad

- **Confidentiality:** Prevent unauthorized disclosure.
- **Integrity:** Prevent unauthorized alteration.
- **Availability:** Keep systems and data accessible when required.

Disclosure, alteration, and destruction describe corresponding adverse outcomes.

## Access control models

Bell–LaPadula focuses on confidentiality through rules commonly summarized as **no read up, no write down**. The strict Biba model focuses on integrity with **no read down, no write up**. These summarize different goals and should not be interchanged.

## Privileged access

Privileged Identity Management concerns the management and lifecycle of privileged identities and roles. Privileged Access Management concerns controlling and monitoring privileged access. Implementations can overlap, but identity governance and access enforcement remain distinct concerns.
