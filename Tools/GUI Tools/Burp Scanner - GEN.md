# Burp Scanner - GEN

Burp Scanner automates application crawling and auditing within supported [[Tools/GUI Tools/BURP Suite - GEN|BURP Suite]] products.

**Scan activities**
---
- Crawling discovers content and navigation paths, including forms and authenticated areas when configured.
- Auditing examines application traffic and behavior for security issues.

Define scope, authentication, and scan configuration before relying on coverage. A crawler can miss functionality it cannot reach or authenticate to.

Use results to guide [[Vulnerability Assessment]], then retain relevant evidence and validate important conclusions. [[Tools/GUI Tools/Burp Repeater - GEN|Burp Repeater]] supports manual follow-up.
